The pictures in this folder are official Minecraft wallpapers published by
Mojang Studios: hero.jpg from the Nether Update, sign-in.jpg from
Wilderness Bound and sign-up.jpg from Tiny Takeover. They keep the size
of the wallpaper files, 2560x1440 and 2048x2048, and are only saved as JPEG:
a smaller copy was drawn larger than itself on a scaled or high-density
screen, and the blocks went soft.
